# Privacy Policy
Version 1 · test release · 12 October 2026

## In short
EduAR has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It does not collect your name, contacts, location, photos, voice or any advertising ID.

## The camera
The camera is used for two things: to recognise your textbook page, and in AR mode to find a table or floor to place the lab on.
The camera picture is processed on your phone, while you use those screens. EduAR never records it, saves it or sends it anywhere.
This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

## Telugu translation
Most Telugu text is built into the app. Text that is not translated yet is translated on your phone by Google ML Kit.
The first time this is needed, the app downloads a Telugu language pack (about 30 MB) from Google. After that, translation works without internet and your text stays on your phone.
Google may receive basic technical information for this download, such as your phone model and app version, under Google's ML Kit terms.

## What stays on your phone
Your settings (language and theme) and any translations made on your phone. They are deleted when you uninstall EduAR or clear its storage in the phone's settings.

## Students
EduAR is made for school students and is meant to be used with a teacher's guidance. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

## Changes
If this policy changes in an important way, the app will show the new version and ask you to agree again.

## Contact
This is a test version. Questions can be raised at github.com/Sri-Ram-007/AR_Virtual_Lab.
